DinePOS Kitchen

The kitchen display, from your DinePOS main PC.

On the main PC: Settings › Kitchen & printers shows it (for example 192.168.1.4).

After connecting

  1. The screen shows a 6-digit code. On the main PC open Settings › Devices, press Approve and enter a manager PIN.
  2. Choose this screen’s station (Kitchen, Tandoor, Bar…). It is remembered.

Keys: Ctrl+Shift+S this page · F5 reload · Ctrl+Shift+M minimize · Ctrl+Shift+Q close
